# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing an open, welcoming, and harassment-free environment for all contributors and maintainers, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Standards of Behavior

- **Be Respectful:** Use inclusive language, accept constructive feedback, and treat others with dignity.
- **Collaborate Constructively:** Focus on technical merit, clean architecture, and what is best for the project.
- **Respect Privacy & Security:** Never disclose sensitive information or private credentials.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. Any concerns may be reported privately to project leads.
